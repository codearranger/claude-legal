description: >-
  Texas employment law subject-matter bundle. Covers Texas Labor Code Chapter 21 (TCHRA —
  Texas Commission on Human Rights Act; TWC charge required within 180 days; mirrors Title
  VII/ADA/ADEA), Texas Payday Law (§ 61.001 — final wages due next regular payday; TWC
  wage claim or civil suit within 2 years), Texas Minimum Wage Act (§ 62.001 — $7.25),
  Texas Non-Solicitation / Non-Compete Act (§ 15.50-15.52: enforceable only if ancillary to
  otherwise-enforceable agreement and reasonable in time/geography/scope; courts may reform
  rather than void), and at-will employment. Wages largely exempt from garnishment
  (Tex. Const. Art. XVI § 28). Triggers on TX discrimination, wage claims, or
  non-compete disputes. Not legal advice.
