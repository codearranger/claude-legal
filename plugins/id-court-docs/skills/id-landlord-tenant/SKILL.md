  Idaho landlord-tenant subject-matter bundle. Covers Idaho Residential Landlord and Tenant
  Act (I.C. § 6-320 et seq. IRLTA), security deposit return (§ 6-321: 21 days after
  possession surrender; itemized deductions; 3x deposit if wrongfully withheld), notice
  requirements for nonpayment (3-day written notice, § 6-303), and FED (unlawful detainer)
  procedure in Magistrate Division (§ 6-301 through 6-324: 3-day summons, trial within
  12 days of answer, appeal to District Court within 42 days). Habitability duty implied
  under Idaho case law (Barlow v. International Harvester Co., 95 Idaho 881 (1974)). 
  Triggers on Idaho eviction defense, security deposit, or habitability claims. Not legal advice.
