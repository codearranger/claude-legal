name: id-employment
description: >-
  Idaho employment law subject-matter bundle. Covers Idaho Human Rights Act (IHRA, I.C. § 67-5901
  et seq. — discrimination in employment; Idaho Human Rights Commission charge required within
  1 year), Idaho Wage Claim Act (§ 45-601 through 45-624 — final wages due within 10 days of
  termination; DOL complaint or civil suit), and at-will employment (strong at-will presumption;
  Hein v. Vining, 151 Idaho 827 (2011)). Non-competes are enforceable in Idaho (§ 44-2701 —
  narrowed by 2008 legislation; must be reasonable; courts disfavor). Idaho has no state WARN
  equivalent; federal 60-day WARN applies. Triggers on ID discrimination, wage claims, or
  non-compete disputes. Not legal advice.
