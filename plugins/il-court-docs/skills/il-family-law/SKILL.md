description: >-
  Illinois family-law subject-matter bundle. Covers dissolution of marriage under IMFL
  (750 ILCS 5): equitable distribution (not community property) of marital vs. non-marital
  property (750 ILCS 5/503), allocation of parental responsibilities (best interests under
  750 ILCS 5/602.5-602.10), child support (IDHFS income-shares guidelines, 750 ILCS 5/505),
  maintenance / spousal support (750 ILCS 5/504), and Order of Protection (IDVA, 750 ILCS 60).
  Triggers on Illinois divorce, custody, child support, or domestic violence proceedings.
  Not legal advice.
