description: >-
  Illinois employment law subject-matter bundle. Covers Illinois Human Rights Act (IHRA,
  775 ILCS 5) with mandatory Illinois Department of Human Rights (IDHR) charge exhaustion
  before circuit court suit (300-day filing deadline), Illinois Wage Payment and Collection
  Act (IWPCA, 820 ILCS 115 — covers final pay, commissions, attorney-fee shifting), Illinois
  Freedom to Work Act (820 ILCS 90 — 2022 reform; non-competes void for employees < $75k;
  non-solicitation agreements void for employees < $45k; 14-day advance-notice requirement),
  Illinois Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act (820 ILCS 65 — 60-day notice),
  and Illinois Paid Leave for All Workers Act (820 ILCS 192 — 2024 effective). Triggers on
  Illinois discrimination, wage claims, non-compete disputes, or WARN Act notices. Not legal advice.
