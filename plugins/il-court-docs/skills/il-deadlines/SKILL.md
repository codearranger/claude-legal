  Illinois civil deadlines skill. Computes deadlines under Ill. Sup. Ct. R. 182 and 735 ILCS

  Christmas; Lincoln's Birthday Feb 12 is an Illinois state holiday). When the last day falls on
  a weekend or holiday, deadline moves to the next court day. Triggers on deadline calculations,
  statute-of-limitations questions, or time-computation in Illinois civil cases. Not legal advice.
