name: il-draft-motion
description: >-
  Illinois motion drafting scaffolder. Produces a blank or partially-filled motion shell
  compliant with Ill. Sup. Ct. R. 131 and the applicable court's local rules: caption,
  introductory paragraph, numbered supporting paragraphs, prayer for relief, certificate of
  service. Distinguishes 735 ILCS 5/2-615 (failure to state claim) vs. 5/2-619 (affirmative
  defense grounds) vs. summary judgment (5/2-1005). Triggers on requests to draft an Illinois
  motion. Not legal advice.
