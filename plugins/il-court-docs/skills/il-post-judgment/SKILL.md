  Illinois post-judgment skill. Covers citation to discover assets (735 ILCS 5/2-1402),
  wage deductions (735 ILCS 5/12-801 et seq.; 85% net take-home exempt for consumer debts
  under state law — note federal CCPA minimum also applies), bank-account garnishment,
  real-property liens (735 ILCS 5/12-101), enforcement of judgments from other states
  (735 ILCS 5/12-650 UEFJA), and judgment vacatur (735 ILCS 5/2-1301, 5/2-1401). Cook County
  citation proceedings at the Daley Center. Triggers on collecting a judgment, garnishment,
  or post-judgment enforcement in Illinois. Not legal advice.
