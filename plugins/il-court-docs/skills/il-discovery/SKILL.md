description: >-
  Illinois civil discovery skill. Governs discovery under Ill. Sup. Ct. R. 201-224: written
  interrogatories (R. 213; 30-interrogatory limit without leave), document requests (R. 214),
  requests to admit (R. 216), depositions (R. 202-206), and expert disclosure (R. 213(f)).
  Covers eDiscovery preservation, proportionality under R. 201(c)(3) (2023 amendment), and
  sanctions (R. 219). Notes Cook County Law Division's mandatory initial discovery disclosures
  (standing order). Triggers on Illinois discovery requests, interrogatory drafting, or
  deposition notices. Not legal advice.
