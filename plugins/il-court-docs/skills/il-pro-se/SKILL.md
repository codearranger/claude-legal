  Illinois pro se civil litigant drafting framework. Guides self-represented litigants through
  filing in Illinois circuit courts: Ill. Sup. Ct. R. 131 format, eFileIL registration and
  fee waiver (735 ILCS 5/5-105), service under Ill. Sup. Ct. R. 11, appearance filing, and
  response deadlines (30 days after service, Ill. Sup. Ct. R. 181). Covers small-claims
  procedure under Ill. Sup. Ct. R. 281-288 (≤ $10,000). Lists available self-help center
  locations (Cook County Law Library, LAF, Prairie State Legal Services). Triggers on
  self-represented Illinois filings or questions about filing without an attorney. Not legal advice.
