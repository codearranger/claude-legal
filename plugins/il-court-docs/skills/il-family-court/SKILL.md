description: >-
  Illinois family court skill. Covers domestic-relations divisions under the Illinois Marriage
  and Dissolution of Marriage Act (IMFL, 750 ILCS 5): dissolution, legal separation, declaration
  of invalidity of marriage, allocation of parental responsibilities (750 ILCS 5/602.5-602.10
  — best interests), child support (IDHFS guidelines, 750 ILCS 5/505), maintenance (750 ILCS
  5/504), and equitable distribution of marital property (non-community-property equitable
  distribution). Order of protection under Illinois Domestic Violence Act (750 ILCS 60).
  Applies to Cook County Domestic Relations Division and county equivalents. Triggers on
  Illinois divorce, custody, child support, maintenance, or domestic violence petition filings.
  Not legal advice.
