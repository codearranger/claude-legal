description: >-
  North Carolina personal-injury subject-matter bundle. Critical distinctive: North Carolina
  is one of four states still applying pure contributory negligence — any plaintiff fault
  completely bars recovery (N.C. Pattern Jury Instr. 101.10; Smith v. Fiber Controls Corp.,
  300 N.C. 669 (1980)). 3-year negligence SOL (§ 1-52(5)), 2-year medical-malpractice SOL
  with 4-year repose (§ 1-15(c)), Rule 9(j) medical-malpractice expert certification
  requirement (§ 1A-1 Rule 9(j)), N.C. Tort Claims Act for governmental defendants
  (§ 143-291), product liability (§ 99B), and wrongful death (§ 28A-18-2). Triggers on
  NC personal injury, negligence, or medical malpractice filings. Not legal advice.
