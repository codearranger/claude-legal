description: >-
  North Carolina civil discovery skill. Governs discovery under N.C.G.S. § 1A-1 Rules 26-37:
  interrogatories (Rule 33; no numerical limit by rule, but courts routinely limit to 30),
  document production (Rule 34), requests to admit (Rule 36), depositions (Rule 30),
  expert disclosure (Rule 26(b)(4)), and protective orders (Rule 26(c)).
  Covers eDiscovery preservation under NC courts' electronic-discovery guidelines and
  sanctions (Rule 37). Triggers on NC discovery requests, interrogatory drafting, or
  deposition notices. Not legal advice.
