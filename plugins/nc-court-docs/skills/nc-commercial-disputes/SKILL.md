description: >-
  North Carolina commercial-disputes subject-matter bundle. Covers NC UCC (§ 25-1 through
  25-9), breach-of-contract SOL (3 years under § 1-52(1) for written K — note, this is
  shorter than many states), UDTPA (§ 75-1.1 — private right of action with treble damages
  and attorney fees; must show trade/commerce nexus), NC Uniform Arbitration Act (§ 1-567.1
  through 1-567.20 NCUAA), business entities (NC Business Corporation Act § 55, NC LLC Act
  § 57D), fraudulent transfer (§ 39-23 NCUFTA), and Business Court (NCBC — complex commercial
  ≥ $5M in controversy or by designation). Triggers on NC commercial litigation, contract
  disputes, UDTPA claims, or business entity disputes. Not legal advice.
