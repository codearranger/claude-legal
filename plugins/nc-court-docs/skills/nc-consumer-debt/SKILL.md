  mandatory 30-day pre-suit letter not required by statute but strategically useful),
  3-year SOL for written contracts (§ 1-52(1)), and integration with FDCPA / FCRA.
  Wage attachment generally unavailable for consumer debts (§ 1-362). Triggers on NC
  debt-collection defense, UDTPA claims, or consumer-debt litigation. Not legal advice.
