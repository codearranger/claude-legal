---
name: nc-draft-motion
description: >-
  North Carolina motion drafting scaffolder. Produces a motion compliant with N.C.G.S.
  § 1A-1 Rule 7 and the applicable court's local rules: caption, basis for motion, supporting
  brief (if required by local rules), proposed order, and certificate of service. Distinguishes
  Rule 12(b) motion to dismiss vs. Rule 56 summary judgment vs. Rule 12(c) judgment on pleadings.
  Triggers on requests to draft an NC motion. Not legal advice.
version: 0.1.0
