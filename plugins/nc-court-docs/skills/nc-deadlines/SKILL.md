name: nc-deadlines
description: >-
  North Carolina civil deadlines skill. Computes deadlines under N.C.G.S. § 1A-1 Rule 6.
  NC legal holidays under § 103-4: New Year's, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Good Friday,
  Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day (observed), Veterans
  Day, Thanksgiving + day after, Christmas. When the last day falls on a weekend or holiday,
  deadline extends to the next business day. SOLs: 3 years contract (§ 1-52(1)); 3 years
  open account (§ 1-52(1)); no separate credit-card rule (treated as written K under § 1-47
  where instrument is signed — 6 years is the minority reading, but § 1-52 governs most cases).
  Triggers on NC deadline calculations or SOL questions. Not legal advice.
