  North Carolina magistrate small-claims court skill. Covers magistrate jurisdiction under
  N.C.G.S. § 7A-210 (civil money claims ≤ $10,000; recovery of personal property). Procedure
  under N.C.G.S. § 7A-211 through § 7A-228: complaint filed with clerk, magistrate hearing
  (informal, no jury), judgment appealable de novo to District Court within 30 days
  (§ 7A-228). Filing fees, service by sheriff, and magistrate's limited discovery powers.
  Triggers on NC small-claims, magistrate court, or claims ≤ $10,000. Not legal advice.
