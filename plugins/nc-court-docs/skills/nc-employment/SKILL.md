name: nc-employment
description: >-
  North Carolina employment law subject-matter bundle. Covers North Carolina Equal Employment
  Practices Act (NCEEPA, § 143-422.2 — provides a state public policy for federal-law-type
  discrimination claims but no private cause of action; primary vehicle is Retaliatory
  Employment Discrimination Act § 95-240 for whistleblower / workers' comp retaliation),
  NC Wage and Hour Act (§ 95-25.1 through 95-25.25: minimum wage $7.25, overtime mirrors
  FLSA, 2-year SOL, Wage and Hour Bureau complaint), NC Workers' Compensation Act (§ 97-1
  et seq., Industrial Commission), at-will employment with public-policy exception (Amos v.
  Oakdale Knitting, 331 N.C. 348 (1992)), and non-compete enforceability (reasonable time/
  geography/scope required; stricter than most states). Triggers on NC wage claims,
  employment discrimination, or non-compete disputes. Not legal advice.
