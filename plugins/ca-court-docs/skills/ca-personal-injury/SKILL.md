  California personal-injury subject-matter bundle. Covers pure comparative fault (Li v.
  Yellow Cab, 13 Cal.3d 804 (1975) — plaintiff can recover even if 99% at fault; damages
  reduced pro rata), Prop 51 (Civ. Code § 1431.2 — joint and several only for economic
  damages; non-economic damages several only), MICRA (CCP § 340.5 — 3 years from injury
  or 1 year from discovery for med-mal; § 3333.2 non-economic damages cap $350,000),
  Barker product liability (20 Cal.3d 413 — consumer expectation + risk-utility tests), and
  Gov. Claims Act (Gov. Code § 945.4 — 6-month claim deadline for government defendants).
  Triggers on CA personal injury or negligence filings. Not legal advice.
