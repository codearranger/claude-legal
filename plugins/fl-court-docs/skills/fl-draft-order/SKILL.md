# Florida Draft Proposed Order

## Florida order structure

Florida proposed orders follow the court's format:

```
[Caption]

              ORDER ON [DEFENDANT/PLAINTIFF]'S
              MOTION TO [RELIEF SOUGHT]

         THIS CAUSE came before the Court on [date] upon
[Party]'s Motion to [Relief Sought] filed on [date], and the
Court having reviewed the motion, any response, the court file,
heard argument of counsel [or the parties], and being otherwise
duly advised in the premises, it is hereby:

         ORDERED AND ADJUDGED that:

         1.  [First decretal paragraph — specific relief granted
             or denied]

         2.  [Additional decretal paragraphs as needed]

         3.  [If applicable: This matter is set for further
             hearing on ________, 20___ at _____ a.m./p.m.]

         DONE AND ORDERED in [City], [County] County, Florida,
this ___ day of __________, 20___.


                                   ___________________________
                                   [JUDGE NAME]
                                   Circuit/County Court Judge
                                   [Judicial Circuit] Judicial Circuit

Copies furnished to:
[List all parties/counsel]
```

## Submission protocol

- Submit proposed orders by email to the JA (judicial
  assistant) after the hearing grants or by leave of court
- Use the court's preferred format (Word for some courts;
  PDF with specific naming conventions for others)
- Always include **"Copies furnished to:"** with all parties
