# Florida — Submit Proposed Order

## After the hearing

1. **Draft the proposed order** promptly (same day or next day
   after the hearing)
2. **Opposing counsel review** (if contested): circulate the
   draft to opposing counsel for approval as to form;
   if they approve, note "Approved as to Form" on the
   submission cover email
3. **Submit to the JA** by email (or through the court's
   submission portal if available)
   - Subject line: `[Case No.] — Proposed Order re [Motion]
     — [Hearing Date]`
   - Attach the proposed order as a Word document (most JAs
     prefer editable format) and/or PDF
   - Copy opposing counsel on the submission email

4. **File-stamped order**: after the judge signs, the JA or
   clerk will return the signed order (as a PDF); file it
   through PACE; serve all parties

## Timing

Submit the proposed order **within 5 business days** of the
hearing. Unreasonable delay can result in the court issuing
its own order.

## If parties disagree on the order

If opposing counsel objects to the form, submit competing
proposed orders and request that the judge resolve the
dispute. Include a cover email explaining the disagreement.
