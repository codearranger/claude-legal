# Florida Draft Motion

## Motion structure

Florida civil motions follow this standard structure:

```
[Caption — see fl-statewide-format]

               DEFENDANT'S MOTION TO [RELIEF SOUGHT]

         Defendant [Name], by and through undersigned counsel [or
pro se], moves this Court for an order [describe relief], and in
support states:

                        INTRODUCTION
[1-2 paragraph summary of the motion and the relief sought]

                   FACTS AND BACKGROUND
[Chronological factual narrative supporting the motion]

                        ARGUMENT

    I. [HEADING FOR FIRST LEGAL ARGUMENT]

    [Argument applying the law to the facts. Florida uses
    fact-pleading — be specific.]

    II. [HEADING FOR SECOND LEGAL ARGUMENT, if any]

    [Argument...]

                       CONCLUSION

         For the foregoing reasons, [Name] respectfully requests
that this Court [specific relief] and grant such other relief as
the Court deems just and proper.

         Respectfully submitted,

         [Date]                    [Signature block]
                                   [Name]
                                   [Address]
                                   [Phone]
                                   [Email]
                                   [Florida Bar No., if attorney]

                      CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE

         I HEREBY CERTIFY that a true and correct copy of the
foregoing was [e-served/mailed] on [date] to: [opposing party/
counsel name and address].

                                   [Signature]
```

## Notice of Hearing

Every noticed motion must be accompanied by or preceded by
a **Notice of Hearing** stating:

- Date and time of hearing
- Location (courtroom/Zoom link)
- Estimated duration
- The motion(s) to be heard

File the Notice of Hearing through PACE simultaneously with or
after the motion.
