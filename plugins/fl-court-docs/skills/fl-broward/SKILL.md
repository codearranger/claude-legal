# Broward Circuit Court (17th Judicial Circuit)

Layer this skill on `fl-statewide-format` for all Broward
Circuit Court civil filings (Fort Lauderdale / Broward County).

## Court location

**Broward County Courthouse**  
201 SE 6th St., Fort Lauderdale, FL 33301  
Civil Division: (954) 831-6565  
Website: `https://www.17th.flcourts.org`

## Civil divisions

Broward assigns civil cases to numbered divisions (e.g., "Civil
Division 23"). The division is random at filing. Check the
Broward Clerk of Courts case inquiry:
`https://www.browardclerk.org/BCC2`

## E-Filing

Through PACE: `https://myflcourtaccess.flcourts.org`
Broward Clerk portal: `https://www.browardclerk.org`

## Broward local rules / Administrative Orders

Administrative Orders: `https://www.17th.flcourts.org/ao`
Key AOs for civil practice include case-management orders,
e-filing requirements, and the Broward CBL program.

## Case management

Broward uses an **active case management** model. After filing,
the court issues a **Case Management Order** setting the
discovery cutoff, expert dates, and trial date within the
first 90-180 days.
