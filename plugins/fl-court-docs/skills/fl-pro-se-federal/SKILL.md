# Florida Federal Courts — Pro Se Overview

Florida has three federal judicial districts:

| District | Divisions | Notable courts |
|---|---|---|
| **S.D. Fla.** (Southern District) | Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Fort Pierce, Key West | Highest civil caseload; complex commercial; FDCPA / FCRA |
| **M.D. Fla.** (Middle District) | Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, Ocala, Fort Myers | Second-busiest; FLSA collective actions; civil rights |
| **N.D. Fla.** (Northern District) | Tallahassee, Pensacola, Gainesville | Lower volume; state-government litigation |

For each district, the governing rules are the **FRCP** + the
district's **Local Rules**. Pro se resources:

- S.D. Fla.: `https://www.flsd.uscourts.gov/representing-yourself`
- M.D. Fla.: `https://www.flmd.uscourts.gov/self-represented-litigants`
- N.D. Fla.: `https://www.flnd.uscourts.gov/self-help`

All three districts have CM/ECF e-filing (PACER-based).
IFP (in forma pauperis) motions are available on Form AO 240.
