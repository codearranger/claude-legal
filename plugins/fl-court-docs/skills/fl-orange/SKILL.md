# Orange County Circuit Court (9th Judicial Circuit)

Layer this skill on `fl-statewide-format` for all Orange County
Circuit Court civil filings (Orlando / Orange County, FL).

## Court location

**Orange County Courthouse**  
425 N. Orange Ave., Orlando, FL 32801  
Civil Division Clerk: (407) 836-2060  
Website: `https://www.ocnjcc.org` (9th JC)

## Civil divisions

Orange County assigns civil cases to numbered divisions. Check
the Orange County Clerk of Courts case inquiry system:
`https://www.myorangeclerk.com`

## E-Filing

Through PACE: `https://myflcourtaccess.flcourts.org`
Orange County Clerk: `https://www.myorangeclerk.com`

## 9th Judicial Circuit local rules

Administrative Orders and local rules:
`https://www.ocnjcc.org/courts/administrative-orders`

The 9th JC has adopted comprehensive case management orders
covering civil, family, and drug court divisions.
