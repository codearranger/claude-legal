# Florida Civil Hearings

## Notice requirements

Florida civil motions require **20 days' notice** before
a hearing unless the rules or the court orders otherwise
(Fla. R. Civ. P. 1.090(e)).

**Emergency/expedited hearing**: motion must show good
cause why the standard notice should be shortened.

## Setting a hearing

1. Contact the **judicial assistant (JA)** of the assigned
   judge to obtain an available hearing date/time
2. File and serve a **Notice of Hearing** stating the
   date, time, location, and matter to be heard
3. Attach the motion to the Notice of Hearing or file
   the motion separately and reference it in the notice

## Uniform motion calendar

Many Florida circuit courts have a **Uniform Motion Calendar
(UMC)** for short motions (typically ≤ 15 minutes).
UMC hearings are self-scheduled through the court's
scheduling system or by contacting the JA. Full-length
hearings require a separate scheduling process.

## Zoom / telephonic hearings

Florida courts widely adopted remote hearings during and
after COVID. Many routine motions (discovery disputes,
case management, simple hearings) are heard by Zoom.
Instructions on the court's or judge's website.

## Courtroom etiquette

- Address the judge as "Your Honor"
- Stand when addressing the court
- Opposing counsel: address as "counsel" not by name
  in argument
- Florida requires counsel to **wear professional
  attire** (no casual clothing in courtroom)
