# Miami-Dade Circuit Court (11th Judicial Circuit)

Layer this skill on `fl-statewide-format` for all Miami-Dade
Circuit Court civil filings.

## Court locations

| Location | Address | Case types |
|---|---|---|
| **Lawson E. Thomas Courthouse Center** | 175 NW 1st Ave., Miami 33128 | General civil (unlimited), complex business |
| **Gerstein Justice Building** | 1351 NW 12th St., Miami 33125 | Criminal; some civil overflow |
| **Dade County Courthouse** | 73 W. Flagler St., Miami 33130 | Limited civil; family; probate |

## Civil divisions

Miami-Dade has multiple **Circuit Civil Divisions** (CA divisions)
and **County Civil Divisions** (CC divisions). Assigned randomly
at filing. The division letter appears in the case number
(e.g., `2024-CA-NNNNN-Division XX`).

## E-Filing

Through PACE: `https://myflcourtaccess.flcourts.org`
Miami-Dade-specific portal: `https://www.miamidadeclerk.com/ocs`

## Miami-Dade Local Rules

Miami-Dade has an extensive set of Administrative Orders
supplementing the Fla. R. Civ. P. Available at:
`https://www.jud11.flcourts.org/Administrative-Orders`

Key AOs for civil practice:
- AO No. 19-02 (e-filing procedures)
- AO No. 20-01 (complex business litigation program)
- Division-specific standing orders (check the assigned
  judge's page)

## Complex Business Litigation Program

Miami-Dade has a **Complex Business Litigation (CBL)** program
for commercial cases meeting the designation criteria. CBL
cases are assigned to a dedicated CBL judge with specialized
case-management procedures.
