# Florida Family Law — Subject-Matter Bundle

> **NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Florida family law was significantly reformed
> in 2023. SB 1416 eliminated permanent alimony effective July 1, 2023.
> Verify current statutory provisions before relying.

## Key framework

- **No-fault divorce only**: irretrievably broken (§ 61.052)
- **Equitable distribution** (not community property): § 61.075
- **Parenting plans required** in all cases with children: § 61.13
- **Alimony reform**: SB 1416 (2023) eliminated permanent alimony
  and restructured alimony types — verify against current § 61.08
- **Residency**: 6 months in Florida before filing (§ 61.021)

## Equitable distribution (§ 61.075)

Court must first classify all assets/liabilities as **marital
or non-marital**, then distribute **equitably** (presumption of
equal division as starting point). Non-marital property generally
excluded. Marital assets include: assets acquired during the
marriage; marital enhancement of non-marital assets; retirement
benefits earned during the marriage.

## Alimony — post-SB 1416 reform (eff. July 1, 2023)

SB 1416 made major changes:
- **Eliminated permanent alimony**
- Types now available: bridge-the-gap (≤ 2 years); rehabilitative;
  durational (time-limited; max 50% of marriage length for marriages
  ≤ 3 yrs; 60% for 3-10 yrs; 75% for 10-20 yrs); temporary (during
  the proceeding)
- **Adultery** remains relevant to alimony determination (§ 61.08(1))

## Parenting plans and time-sharing (§ 61.13)

Every dissolution involving minor children requires a parenting plan
describing: the time-sharing schedule; responsibility for major
decisions (parental responsibility, default = shared); child's
school, activities, health care.

**Best interests of the child** factors: § 61.13(3) (20 statutory
factors including history of domestic violence; willingness to
facilitate relationship with other parent; moral fitness; stability
of home environments; preference of child if sufficient maturity).

## Child support guidelines (§ 61.30)

**Income shares model**. The Worksheet (Form 12.902(e)) calculates
support from both parents' net monthly incomes, the overnight
time-sharing schedule, health insurance costs, and child-care costs.
Guideline amount is presumptively correct; deviation requires
written findings.

## Domestic violence injunction (§ 741.30)

Petition for Injunction for Protection Against Domestic Violence
using Form 12.980(a). Ex parte temporary injunction issued same-day
if immediate danger shown. Full hearing within **15 days** (§ 741.30(5)).
Final injunction: up to 1 year, renewable indefinitely.

**Firearms**: federal 18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(8) applies to prohibit
firearms possession once a qualifying injunction issues.
