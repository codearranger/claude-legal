# Florida Draft Notice

## Notice of Hearing

```
[Caption]

                    NOTICE OF HEARING

TO:  [Opposing party/counsel name and address]

         PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the undersigned will call up for
hearing the [Motion filed on date] before the Honorable [Judge
Name], [County] County Circuit/County Court, [Division], located
at [Courthouse Address], [City], Florida, on [Day, Date] at
[Time], or as soon thereafter as counsel may be heard.

         The estimated time for this hearing is [X] minutes/hours.

         [If Zoom: The hearing will be conducted via Zoom. Meeting
         ID: _____. Password: _____.]

         [Date]                     Respectfully submitted,

                                    [Signature block]

                        CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE
[Standard Florida certificate of service]
```

## Notice of Appearance

```
[Caption]

                  NOTICE OF APPEARANCE

         [Attorney/Pro Se Party Name], hereby enters a Notice of
Appearance on behalf of [Plaintiff/Defendant], [Party Name], in
the above-captioned matter, and requests that copies of all
pleadings, motions, orders, and notices be served upon the
undersigned.

         [Date]                     [Signature block]
                                    [Florida Bar No., if attorney]
```
