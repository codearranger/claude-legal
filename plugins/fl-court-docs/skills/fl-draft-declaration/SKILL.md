# Florida Draft Affidavit / Declaration

## Florida uses affidavits (sworn before notary)

Unlike federal practice (28 U.S.C. § 1746 unsworn
declarations), Florida courts primarily use **affidavits**
sworn before a notary public. An unsworn declaration
under Fla. Stat. § 92.525 is available for some purposes
but is not universally accepted in lieu of a sworn
affidavit for summary judgment or motions requiring
sworn evidence.

## Standard Florida affidavit structure

```
[Caption]

           AFFIDAVIT OF [AFFIANT NAME] IN SUPPORT OF
           [MOTION / RESPONSE]

STATE OF FLORIDA          )
COUNTY OF [COUNTY]        )

         Before me, the undersigned authority, personally appeared
[AFFIANT NAME], who, being first duly sworn, deposes and says:

    1.  I am [relationship to case, e.g., "the Defendant in the
        above-captioned matter"] and I make this affidavit based
        upon my personal knowledge.

    2.  [Factual paragraph]

    3.  [Additional factual paragraphs, numbered sequentially]

         FURTHER AFFIANT SAYETH NAUGHT.

_______________________________
[AFFIANT NAME]

         Sworn to and subscribed before me this ___ day of
________, 20___, by [AFFIANT NAME], who is [personally known
to me / produced ______________ as identification].

_______________________________
Notary Public, State of Florida
Print Name: ___________________
Commission No.: _______________
Commission Expires: ___________
[NOTARY SEAL]
```

## E-signing affidavits

Florida has adopted electronic notarization (Ron — Remote
Online Notarization): Fla. Stat. §§ 117.201-117.265.
RON is valid for most civil-practice purposes.
