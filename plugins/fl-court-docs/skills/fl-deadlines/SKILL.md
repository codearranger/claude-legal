# Florida Civil Deadline Arithmetic

## Rule 1.090 — Time Computation

Florida time computation rules (Rule 1.090(a)):
- **Exclude** the day of the event
- **Include** the last day (but if the last day is
  Saturday, Sunday, or a legal holiday, extend to
  the next business day)
- For periods ≤ 7 days: **exclude intermediate
  Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays**
- Electronic service adds **2 days** to deadlines
  counted from service

## Florida State Holidays (Fla. Stat. § 110.117)

Florida recognizes the following legal holidays:

1. New Year's Day (Jan 1)
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day (3rd Mon Jan)
3. Memorial Day (last Mon May)
4. Independence Day (Jul 4)
5. Labor Day (1st Mon Sep)
6. Veterans Day (Nov 11)
7. Thanksgiving Day (4th Thu Nov)
8. Christmas Day (Dec 25)

**Note**: Florida does not observe Columbus Day,
Presidents' Day, or Juneteenth as mandatory state
holidays (§ 110.117). Some state agencies may observe
additional holidays. Individual counties may also close
for local holidays — check the specific court's
holiday closure calendar.

## Key Statutes of Limitations (§ 95.11)

| Claim type | SOL |
|---|---|
| Written contract | **5 years** (§ 95.11(2)(b)) |
| Oral contract | **4 years** (§ 95.11(3)(k)) |
| Negligence / personal injury | **4 years** (§ 95.11(3)(a)) |
| Wrongful death | **2 years** (§ 95.11(4)(d)) |
| Fraud | **4 years** from discovery (§ 95.11(3)(j)) |
| UCC (goods) | **5 years** (§ 95.11(2)(b) / § 672.725) |
| FDCPA | **1 year** |
| FCCPA | **2 years** (§ 559.77(4)) |
| FDUTPA | **4 years** (§ 501.2077) |

## Answer / response deadlines

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Answer to complaint | 20 days from service (Rule 1.140(a)) |
| Reply to motion | 15 days from service of motion |
| Notice period for regular motion | 20 days (Rule 1.090(e)) |
| PACE midnight filing | Generally 11:59 p.m. local time |
