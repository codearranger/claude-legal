# Florida County Courts — Roll-Up

## County court jurisdiction (Fla. Stat. § 34.01)

Florida county courts have **civil jurisdiction up to $50,000**.
The county court is the primary forum for:
- Consumer-debt collection actions (the majority of debt
  collection suits in Florida are ≤ $50,000)
- Landlord-tenant (evictions and security-deposit disputes)
- Small-claims cases (≤ $8,000, under the Florida Small
  Claims Rules)

## Small Claims Rules (Fla. R. Sm. Cl. Ct.)

Cases ≤ $8,000 proceed under the Small Claims Rules:
- Filing uses the **Statement of Claim** (not a formal
  complaint)
- Service by mail is available
- Pre-trial conference / mediation typically required
  before trial
- Discovery is limited; parties may conduct informal
  discovery with leave of court
