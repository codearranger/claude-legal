  Colorado employment law subject-matter bundle. Covers Colorado Anti-Discrimination Act
  (CADA, § 24-34-402 — state analog to Title VII/ADA; CCRD charge required within 300 days;
  private right of action after right-to-sue), Colorado COMPS Order (7 CCR 1103-1 — minimum
  wage, overtime, rest breaks), Colorado Equal Pay for Equal Work Act (§ 8-5-101 — 2021
  effective; pay-range disclosure required in job postings; 3-year SOL), Colorado Protect
  Whistleblowers Act (§ 24-114-102), HFWA (Healthy Families and Workplaces Act, § 8-13.3-401
  — 48 hours accrued paid sick leave), and non-compete limits (§ 8-2-113: enforceable only
  for professionals earning ≥ $123,750 (2024 threshold)). Triggers on CO discrimination,
  wage claims, or non-compete disputes. Not legal advice.
