# Pennsylvania Family Law — Subject-Matter Bundle

> **NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Refer to pa-family-court for the
> full procedural framework. This bundle provides the
> substantive-law framework for family-law matters.

This skill is the subject-matter companion to the
`pa-family-court` venue skill. It adds:

- **Equitable distribution factors** (23 Pa. C.S. § 3502):
  length of marriage; prior marriages; age, health, and
  employability; sources of income; estate values; standard
  of living; tax consequences; economic circumstances at
  time of distribution; whether the party will be the
  custodian of minor children; contributions as homemaker;
  liabilities; needs of the parties
- **Alimony** (§ 3701): 17 factors; no automatic award;
  rehabilitative alimony most common; alimony pendente
  lite available during proceedings
- **Custody best-interests factors** (§ 5328): 16 factors;
  domestic violence must be considered as primary factor
  when relevant
- **Child support guidelines**: both parents' net incomes
  + schedule of basic support obligations + adjustments
  for health insurance, child care, and other expenses
- **PFA substantive standards** (§ 6101): "abuse" defined
  broadly (physical assault, false imprisonment,
  harassment, stalking, rape); preponderance of evidence
