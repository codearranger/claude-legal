# Pennsylvania Civil Deadline Arithmetic

## Pa. R.C.P. 106 — Time Computation

- **Exclude** the day of the event
- **Include** the last day; if the last day falls on a
  Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, extend to the
  next business day
- For periods ≤ 7 days: exclude intermediate Saturdays,
  Sundays, and holidays

## Pennsylvania State Holidays (44 Pa. C.S. § 11)

Pennsylvania recognizes:
1. New Year's Day (Jan 1)
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day (3rd Mon Jan)
3. Presidents' Day (3rd Mon Feb)
4. Good Friday (Friday before Easter)
5. Memorial Day (last Mon May)
6. Juneteenth (June 19)
7. Independence Day (Jul 4)
8. Labor Day (1st Mon Sep)
9. Columbus Day (2nd Mon Oct)
10. Veterans Day (Nov 11)
11. Thanksgiving Day (4th Thu Nov)
12. Christmas Day (Dec 25)

**Note**: Good Friday is a Pennsylvania legal holiday —
this is distinctive; many states do not observe it.

## Statutes of Limitations (42 Pa. C.S.)

| Claim type | SOL |
|---|---|
| Personal injury; fraud; wrongful death | **2 years** (§ 5524) |
| Written contracts; UCC goods; trespass to property | **4 years** (§ 5525) |
| Oral contracts; general residual | **6 years** (§ 5527) |
| FDCPA | **1 year** |
| UTPCPL | **6 years** (§ 5527(b)(2)); or 2 years from discovery |
| FCEUA | **6 years** or 2 years from discovery |

## Key procedural deadlines

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Answer to complaint | 20 days from service (Pa. R.C.P. 1026) |
| Response to preliminary objections | 20 days |
| MDJ appeal to CCP | 30 days from MDJ judgment |
| Motion for summary judgment (typical) | Varies by local rule; usually after pleadings close |
