# Pennsylvania — Schedule a Hearing

## General process

Scheduling a motion for hearing in Pennsylvania CCP varies
significantly by county. Always check the specific court's
local rules and the judge's individual court practices.

### Philadelphia CCP
- **Motion Court**: some motions are decided in motion court
  without a separate scheduled hearing
- Contact the courtroom deputy of the assigned judge for
  full hearings

### Allegheny County
- Motions listed on the court's **Argument List**
  (published by the Prothonotary)
- Contact the judge's chambers to confirm scheduling

### Most other CCPs
1. File the motion (and response/reply per the briefing
   schedule)
2. Contact the judge's chambers or the Prothonotary to
   list the motion for **argument**
3. Argument dates are typically set monthly

## Praecipe to List for Argument

In some counties, a **Praecipe to List for Argument** is
filed to request that the motion be placed on the argument
list. Verify the specific county practice.

## MDJ hearings

MDJ hearings are set by the MDJ at the time of filing. The
MDJ's office notifies all parties of the date and time.
No separate scheduling is required.
