# Pennsylvania — Submit Proposed Order

After the court rules on a motion, the prevailing party
typically prepares and submits a proposed order.

## Submission methods

1. **PACFile**: upload the proposed order as a PDF through
   PACFile; tag it as a "Proposed Order"
2. **Chambers email**: many judges prefer the proposed order
   emailed directly to chambers in Word format so the judge
   can edit — check the judge's standing orders
3. **Mail to Prothonotary**: acceptable in some counties

## Coordination with opposing counsel

Before submitting a proposed order, circulate the draft to
opposing counsel and ask them to approve as to form. If
they approve, note "Approved as to Form" in the submission
cover email.

If there is a dispute about the form of the order, submit
competing proposed orders with a brief explanation of the
dispute.

## Timing

Submit within **5 business days** of the ruling. Delay can
result in the court issuing its own order.
