# Pennsylvania Fact-Check

## Checklist

### Format
- [ ] Caption: `IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS OF [COUNTY] COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA`
- [ ] Division included (Civil, Family, Orphans' Court)
- [ ] Docket number in correct format for the county
- [ ] **Verification** signed by the party under 18 Pa. C.S. § 4904
  (unless the pleading is exempt from the verification requirement)
- [ ] Filed through PACFile (or confirmed paper filing is permitted)

### Citations
- [ ] Every Pennsylvania case citation is to a Pennsylvania
  official reporter (Pa., Pa. Super., Pa. Cmwlth.) with year
- [ ] Statutory cites use Pa. C.S. format or P.S. for unconsolidated
  statutes
- [ ] Rules cited as Pa. R.C.P. (not Pa. R. Civ. P.)

### Timeliness
- [ ] SOL has not run (verify applicable § 5524-5527 period)
- [ ] For MDJ appeal: 30-day window from judgment not expired
- [ ] For PFA: 10 business days from ex parte order to full hearing

### Consumer-debt cases
- [ ] FCEUA applicability checked (applies to creditors collecting
  their own debts — broader than FDCPA)
- [ ] UTPCPL § 201-9.2 unfair or deceptive act element verified
- [ ] Chain of title documents identified and authenticated
