# Pennsylvania Consumer-Debt Defense

> **NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Pennsylvania's consumer-protection
> statutes are powerful but have specific procedural
> requirements. Verify all SOLs and procedural steps against
> current Pennsylvania statutes before filing.

## Pennsylvania-specific consumer-protection framework

### FCEUA (Fair Credit Extension Uniformity Act) — 73 P.S. § 2270.1

The FCEUA is Pennsylvania's analogue to the FDCPA but with a
key distinction: it applies to **both original creditors and
debt collectors**. Key provisions:
- **Unfair or deceptive debt collection**: § 2270.4 incorporates
  the same prohibitions as the FDCPA (cross-reference to
  FDCPA § 1692b-1692j) and makes them applicable to
  **all persons** collecting consumer debt
- **Private right of action**: through the UTPCPL, not directly;
  a FCEUA violation is a per se UTPCPL violation (§ 2270.5(a))
- **SOL**: 2 years under 42 Pa. C.S. § 5524; or 6 years under
  the UTPCPL's statute (§ 5527) — courts have split; verify

### UTPCPL (Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law) — 73 P.S. § 201-1

The UTPCPL provides powerful remedies:
- **Unfair methods of competition** or **deceptive acts** in
  trade or commerce
- Private right of action: actual damages; **treble damages**
  (§ 201-9.2) for intentional conduct; mandatory attorney's fees
  for prevailing plaintiff
- Broadly used in consumer-debt defense to attack deceptive
  collection practices

## Chain of title — Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania courts apply the same chain-of-title requirements
as other states. The business-records foundation under Pa. R.E.
803(6) requires: (1) the record was made at or near the time of
the event; (2) by a person with knowledge; (3) in the course of
a regularly conducted business activity; (4) it was the regular
practice to make such records.

For debt buyers, the **foundational challenge** focuses on the
inability of the debt buyer's custodian to authenticate the
original creditor's records — they cannot testify that the
records were made in the original creditor's regular course of
business.

## MDJ defense tactics

Consumer-debt suits at the MDJ level (≤ $12,000) have
informal procedures. Effective MDJ defenses:
- Request continuance to obtain documentation from plaintiff
- Challenge the plaintiff's standing / chain of title at hearing
- Assert FDCPA / FCEUA violations as counterclaims
- If MDJ enters default: file timely CCP appeal (30 days)
  and assert all defenses in the de novo CCP proceeding
