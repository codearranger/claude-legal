# Pennsylvania Draft Notice

## Notice of Service of Discovery

```
IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS OF [COUNTY] COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

[Caption]

              NOTICE OF SERVICE OF [DISCOVERY DOCUMENT]

         Please take notice that, on [date], [Party Name] served
[Interrogatories / Requests for Production / Requests for
Admission / Notice of Deposition] upon all parties of record by
[method of service].

Date: ______________   ____________________________
                       [Name]
                       [Address / Contact Info]

                       CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE
[Standard Pennsylvania certificate of service]
```

## Notice of Deposition (Pa. R.C.P. 4007.1)

```
[Caption]

              NOTICE OF ORAL DEPOSITION

         PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that, pursuant to Pa. R.C.P. 4007.1,
the deposition of [DEPONENT NAME] will be taken upon oral
examination before a Notary Public or other officer authorized
to take depositions, at [address], on [date], commencing at
[time]. The deposition will be recorded [stenographically / by
video / by both means].

         You are invited to attend and cross-examine.

Date: ______________   ____________________________
                       [Name]
```
