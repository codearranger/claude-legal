# Allegheny County Court of Common Pleas (Fifth Judicial District)

The Allegheny County CCP (Pittsburgh) is Pennsylvania's second-
largest court of common pleas.

## Court location

**City-County Building**  
414 Grant Street, Pittsburgh, PA 15219  
Civil Division Clerk: (412) 350-5300  
Website: `https://www.alleghenycourtrecords.us.com` (case search)
         `https://www.alleghenycounty.us/courts` (court home)

## Civil divisions

Allegheny County civil cases are assigned to numbered civil
judges. The **Commerce and Complex Litigation Center** (CCLC)
handles designated complex commercial matters.

## Allegheny County Local Rules

Local Rules available at:
`https://www.alleghenycourts.us/civil/rules.aspx`

Key provisions:
- Discovery motions must include a certification of good-faith
  effort to resolve the dispute
- Summary judgment briefing schedule set by individual judge's
  practices
- Expert witness procedures supplemented by local rule

## E-Filing (PACFile)

Allegheny County civil cases use PACFile for e-filing.
`https://ujsportal.pacourts.us`
