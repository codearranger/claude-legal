# Pennsylvania Draft Motion

## Pennsylvania motion structure

```
IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS OF [COUNTY] COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

[CIVIL DIVISION]

[PLAINTIFF NAME],
                    Plaintiff,
                                        No. [YYYY-NNNNN]
             v.

[DEFENDANT NAME],
                    Defendant.


      DEFENDANT'S MOTION [TITLE — e.g., FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT]

         Defendant, [Name], by and through undersigned counsel
[or pro se], respectfully moves this Honorable Court for
[relief sought], and in support thereof states as follows:

                        INTRODUCTION
[Brief statement of the motion and relief sought]

                   STATEMENT OF FACTS
[Numbered factual paragraphs; attach exhibits if needed]

                        ARGUMENT

I.   [HEADING FOR FIRST LEGAL ARGUMENT]

     A.  [Subheading if applicable]

     [Argument applying Pennsylvania law to the facts]

II.  [HEADING FOR SECOND LEGAL ARGUMENT]

     [Argument...]

                       CONCLUSION

         WHEREFORE, Defendant respectfully requests that this
Honorable Court enter an order [specific relief] and grant such
other relief as is just and appropriate.

                               Respectfully submitted,

Date: ______________           ____________________________
                               [Name]
                               [Address]
                               [Phone]
                               [Pa. Bar No. if attorney]

                      CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE

         I hereby certify that on [date], I served a true and
correct copy of the foregoing upon [opposing party/counsel] by
[method of service — e.g., first-class mail, PACFile electronic
service].

                               ____________________________
                               [Name]
```
