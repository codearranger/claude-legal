# Pennsylvania Statewide Pleading Format

Pennsylvania pleading format is governed by the **Pennsylvania
Rules of Civil Procedure (Pa. R.C.P.)** and county-level local
rules.

## Caption format

```
IN THE COURT OF COMMON PLEAS OF [COUNTY] COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

[CIVIL DIVISION / FAMILY COURT DIVISION / etc.]

[PLAINTIFF NAME],
                    Plaintiff,
                                        No. [YYYY-NNNNN]
             v.
                                        [Type of action]
[DEFENDANT NAME],
                    Defendant.
```

**Pa. R.C.P. 1018**: every pleading must be captioned with
the court, the county, the names of the parties, and the
docket number.

## Signature and verification

**Pa. R.C.P. 1024** (Verification): most pleadings in
Pennsylvania require a **verification** — a sworn statement
that the facts stated are true and correct. Verifications are
signed by the party (not the attorney) under penalty of 18
Pa. C.S. § 4904.

The standard verification block:
```
VERIFICATION
I verify that the statements made in this [pleading] are true
and correct. I understand that false statements herein are made
subject to the penalties of 18 Pa. C.S. § 4904, relating to
unsworn falsification to authorities.

Date: ____________    _________________________
                      [Party Name]
```

**Pa. R.C.P. 1023.1-1023.4** (Attorney's Certification): the
attorney signature on every document certifies that the document
is not frivolous and is supported by a reasonable inquiry.

## PACFile — mandatory e-filing

Pennsylvania mandates electronic filing through **PACFile**
for most civil cases:
`https://ujsportal.pacourts.us`

- Registration required (free)
- Paper filing still available for pro se litigants in many
  counties
- Filing cut-off: verify with specific county — most accept
  filings through PACFile until **11:59 p.m.** for same-day
  processing (docketed the next business day)

## Pennsylvania docket number format

`[YYYY]-[NNNNNN]-[type]` (e.g., `2024-001234-CV` for civil).
County courts may use different formats. Verify the exact
format for the county.
