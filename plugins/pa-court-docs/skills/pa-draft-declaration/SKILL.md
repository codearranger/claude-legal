# Pennsylvania Draft Verification / Affidavit

## Verification (for pleadings — Pa. R.C.P. 1024)

Used within a pleading or as a standalone verification:

```
VERIFICATION

I, [Name], verify that the statements made in this [pleading/
motion] are true and correct to the best of my knowledge,
information, and belief. I understand that false statements
herein are made subject to the penalties of 18 Pa. C.S. §
4904, relating to unsworn falsification to authorities.

Date: ____________    _______________________________
                      [Party Name]
```

## Affidavit (sworn before a notary)

For formal affidavits submitted as evidence:

```
AFFIDAVIT OF [AFFIANT NAME]

I, [Name], being duly sworn according to law, depose and say:

1.  I am [relationship to case].

2.  [Numbered factual paragraphs...]

3.  ...

I verify that the foregoing statements are true and correct.
I understand that false statements herein are made subject to
the penalties of 18 Pa. C.S. § 4904.

                          _______________________________
                          [Affiant Name]

Sworn and subscribed before me this ___ day of _______, 20___.

                          _______________________________
                          Notary Public
                          My commission expires: _________
```
