# Pennsylvania Magisterial District Judge (MDJ)

The MDJ tier is the entry-level court in Pennsylvania for:
- **Civil claims ≤ $12,000** (small claims / "civil complaint")
- **Landlord-tenant matters** (possession + rent + damages;
  any amount for possession)
- **Summary criminal offenses**

The MDJ is the **primary forum** for consumer-debt collection
in Pennsylvania — most debt-buyer suits and original-creditor
collection actions start here because they are ≤ $12,000.

## Governing rules

**Pennsylvania Rules of Civil Procedure before Magisterial
District Judges (Pa. R.C.P.M.D.J.)** — available at:
`https://www.pacourts.us/rules/rules-of-civil-procedure-before-magisterial-district-judges`

## Procedure — civil complaint

1. **File the Civil Complaint** with the MDJ for the district
   where the defendant resides or where the contract was
   made (Pa. R.C.P.M.D.J. Rule 302)
2. **Filing fee** (varies by claim amount)
3. **MDJ serves** the complaint on the defendant by
   first-class mail (and constable service if requested)
4. **Hearing date** set by the MDJ (typically 20-35 days
   out)
5. **Hearing**: both parties appear; informal rules of
   evidence; MDJ decision on the spot or within a few days
6. **Judgment**: if defendant does not appear → default;
   if plaintiff does not appear → non-pros

**Appeal**: any party may appeal **within 30 days** of
entry of the MDJ judgment to the Court of Common Pleas
(de novo — new trial on the merits). Appeal costs: filing
fee in the CCP.

## Landlord-tenant / Possession matters

For eviction (possession):
1. Serve **written notice to quit** (or notice to vacate)
   — the required notice period depends on the reason
   for termination and the lease terms
2. File a **Complaint for Possession** with the MDJ for
   the district where the property is located
3. MDJ schedules a hearing (typically 7-15 days after
   service of the complaint)
4. If MDJ grants judgment for possession: **Order for
   Possession** issued; 10 days for the tenant to vacate
5. If tenant does not vacate within 10 days: **Writ of
   Possession** → constable or sheriff to execute

**De novo appeal**: tenant may appeal to the CCP within
30 days of MDJ judgment. Appeal automatically stays the
Writ of Possession pending the CCP proceeding (tenant
must pay current and future rent to the CCP escrow to
maintain the stay).
