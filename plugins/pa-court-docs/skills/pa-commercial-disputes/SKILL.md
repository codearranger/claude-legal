# Pennsylvania Commercial Disputes — Subject-Matter Bundle

> **NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Pennsylvania's UTPCPL is available
> in some B2B disputes. Fact-pleading requirements are strict.
> Verify all cites against current Pennsylvania statutes and
> case law before filing.

## Breach of contract

- **4-year SOL** for written contracts (42 Pa. C.S. § 5525)
- **6-year SOL** for oral contracts (§ 5527)
- **UCC** (goods): 4 years from breach (13 Pa. C.S. § 2725)
- **Fact-pleading**: Pennsylvania requires pleading of all
  ultimate facts; conclusory allegations are subject to
  demurrer (preliminary objections)

### Attorney's fees

Pennsylvania follows the **American Rule** strictly —
fees are not recoverable absent a statute or contract.
Key fee provisions:
- **UTPCPL**: mandatory attorney's fees for prevailing
  plaintiff (73 P.S. § 201-9.2)
- **WPCL**: fees for prevailing employee (43 Pa. C.S. § 260.9a)
- Contractual fee clauses are enforced

## UTPCPL (73 P.S. § 201-1) in commercial disputes

UTPCPL applies to B2B unfair or deceptive trade practices.
The **catch-all provision** (§ 201-2(4)(xxi) — "any other
fraudulent or deceptive conduct") is the broadest ground.
Plaintiff must show:
1. Deceptive conduct by the defendant
2. Justifiable reliance
3. Ascertainable loss

**Treble damages** (up to 3x actual damages) available for
intentional violations (§ 201-9.2(a)).

## Pennsylvania Business Corporation Law (BCL) — 15 Pa. C.S. § 1101

Key litigation provisions:
- **Derivative suits** (§ 1782-1783): demand requirement;
  special litigation committee defense
- **Shareholder inspection rights** (§ 1508)
- **Judicial dissolution** (§ 1983): for deadlock, oppression,
  illegal activity, or fraud; a powerful minority-shareholder
  remedy
- **Business judgment rule**: directors protected for good-
  faith, informed decisions in the best interests of the
  corporation (§ 1715 — Pennsylvania's version considers a
  broader constituency including stakeholders)

## Pennsylvania LLC Act (15 Pa. C.S. § 8811 et seq.)

Pennsylvania adopted the Pennsylvania Uniform Limited
Liability Company Act (PULLCA) in 2016 (eff. 2017):
- **Fiduciary duties** (§ 8849.1): duty of loyalty +
  duty of care; operating agreement can adjust
- **Judicial dissolution** (§ 8878): for member deadlock,
  waste, oppression, or impracticability of continuing
- **Member dissociation and buyout** (§ 8861 et seq.)

## Pennsylvania Arbitration Act (42 Pa. C.S. § 7302)

Pennsylvania's own Arbitration Act applies to domestic
arbitration agreements. FAA also applies to contracts
involving commerce (broad). Key features:
- Motion to compel arbitration (§ 7304)
- No separate Pennsylvania unconscionability standard
  more restrictive than federal — but equitable defenses
  (fraud, duress) available
- Judicial review: very limited (§ 7314 — corruption,
  evident partiality, exceeding powers, procedural
  misconduct)
