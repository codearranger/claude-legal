# Philadelphia Court of Common Pleas (First Judicial District)

The Philadelphia CCP is the largest Court of Common Pleas in
Pennsylvania and one of the busiest trial courts in the country.
It has specialized programs including the Commerce Court.

## Court locations

| Division | Location |
|---|---|
| **Civil Trial Division** | City Hall, Broad and Market Streets, Philadelphia 19107 |
| **Commerce Court** | City Hall, Room 296 |
| **Family Court Division** | 1501 Arch Street, Philadelphia |
| **Common Pleas Criminal** | Criminal Justice Center, 1301 Filbert Street |

Clerk of Courts: (215) 686-6670  
Website: `https://courts.phila.gov`

## Civil docket types

- **Court of Common Pleas** (CV — civil): unlimited civil
  jurisdiction for claims over $12,000
- **Municipal Court** (separate court for claims ≤ $12,000;
  Small Claims ≤ $12,000): `https://courts.phila.gov/municipal/`
- **Commerce Court**: designated for commercial cases ≥ $50,000
  involving business entities; streamlined management

## Philadelphia Local Rules

Philadelphia has extensive local rules that supplement the
Pa. R.C.P. Available at:
`https://courts.phila.gov/rules/`

Key local rules:
- **Phila. L.R. Civ. P. 208.3**: motions practice and briefing
- **Phila. L.R. Civ. P. 4005**: discovery procedures
- **Commerce Court** local rules (separate track)
- **Mass Tort Program** rules (asbestos, pharmaceuticals, etc.)

## Case Management

Philadelphia uses **active case management** through the
Philadelphia Civil Administrative Judge's program. Cases
are tracked through mandatory status conferences.

## E-Filing (PACFile)

Philadelphia CCP requires electronic filing through PACFile
(`https://ujsportal.pacourts.us`) for civil cases. Pro se
litigants may file in paper at the Clerk of Courts window.
