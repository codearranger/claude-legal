  Georgia employment law subject-matter bundle. Covers Georgia Fair Employment Practices Act
  (O.C.G.A. § 45-19-20 — applies only to state-agency employees; private employers governed
  by Title VII/ADA/ADEA), Georgia Equal Pay for Equal Work Act (§ 34-5-3), Georgia Wage
  Payment Act (§ 34-7-2 — wages due on regular paydays; no liquidated damages provision
  unlike many states), Restrictive Covenants Act (§ 13-8-50 through 13-8-58 — 2011 reform;
  non-competes enforceable if reasonable time ≤ 2 years + geographic + scope; courts reform
  rather than void), and at-will employment. Triggers on GA discrimination, wage claims,
  non-compete disputes, or wrongful termination. Not legal advice.
