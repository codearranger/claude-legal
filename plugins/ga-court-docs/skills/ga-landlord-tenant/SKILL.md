  State Court (§ 44-7-50 through 44-7-59: answer within 7 days; trial within 30 days),
  habitability (§ 44-7-13 — implied warranty), deposit escrow requirements (§ 44-7-31),
  and retaliatory eviction defense (§ 44-7-24). Note: dispossessory is filed in Magistrate
  Court; post-judgment garnishment is a separate proceeding under Title 18 ch. 4.
  Triggers on GA eviction defense, security deposit, or habitability claims. Not legal advice.
