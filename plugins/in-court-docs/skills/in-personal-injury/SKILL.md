  Medical Malpractice Act (IC 34-18 — $1.65M cap; mandatory preliminary panel of 3 doctors

  delivery), and wrongful death (IC 34-23-1-1). Triggers on IN personal injury, negligence,
  or med-mal claims. Not legal advice.
