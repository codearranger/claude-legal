description: >-
  Indiana employment law subject-matter bundle. Covers Indiana Civil Rights Law (IC 22-9-1 —
  discrimination in employment; ICRC charge required within 180 days; civil right of action
  2 years from violation), Indiana Wage Payment Statute (IC 22-2-5: final wages due on next
  regular payday; 2x liquidated damages + attorney fees if employer fails to pay), Indiana
  Wage Claims Statute (IC 22-2-9: DOL complaint or civil suit), Indiana WARN Act (no state
  WARN — federal 60-day WARN applies), and at-will employment with public-policy exception
  (Frampton v. Central Indiana Gas Co., 260 Ind. 249 (1973)). Triggers on IN discrimination,
  wage claims, or wrongful termination. Not legal advice.
